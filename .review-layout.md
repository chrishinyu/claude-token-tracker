# $impeccable — LAYOUT assessment (PRODUCT register)
Target: `popup/popup.html` (inline `<style>`), `popup/popup.js`. Assessment only; no edits made.

## 1. Spacing — consistent or arbitrary?
**Mostly arbitrary at the small end.** A 6-step scale is declared (`:root` L62, `--sp-1..6` = 4/8/12/16/20/28) but is bypassed by ~12 raw values, all in the 1–6px band where rhythm is actually decided:
- `.meter-val-row` L188 — `gap: 5px; margin-bottom: 4px` (5 is on no scale)
- `.meter-verdict` L193 — `margin: 0 0 4px`
- `.sec-h` L234 — `gap: 6px`; same 6px in `.extra-active-label`, `.settings-toggle`
- `.spark-bars` L276 `gap: 3px`; `.spark-days` L291 `margin-top: 3px`; `.hourly-bars` L336 `gap: 1px`; `.hourly-labels` L343 `margin-top: 2px`
- `.export-btn` L318 `padding: 2px 0`; `.settings-row` L357 `padding: 4px 0`; `.settings-select` L363 `padding: 3px 6px`; `.settings-hint` L374 `margin-top: 2px`
Chart-internal values (spark gap 3, hourly gap 1) are legitimately off-scale — bar gaps are a data-density decision, not layout. The rest (4/5/6/2/3px paddings on text rows) are not.

**Scale itself is flawed:** `--sp-5: 20` and `--sp-6: 28` break the doubling logic (4/8/12/16 → should continue 24/32). Neither is used except in `.auth-state`/`.empty-state` padding (L393) and the two are used together there, so the odd steps buy nothing.

**Meter tile internal rhythm is monotone-then-arbitrary.** Reading down `#meterTile`:
number → verdict `4px` → countdown `4px` → bar `12px` → foot `12px` → updated `4px` → advisory `8px`.
The 42px hero and the 13px verdict are separated by the *same* 4px as verdict→countdown. Three unrelated facts (state word, reset clock, timestamp) are spaced identically to the hero's own descender gap, so nothing reads as a unit. There is no group boundary — bar and foot are structurally one object (track + its axis labels) yet are split by the same `--sp-3` that separates them from everything else (`.bar-track` L212 `margin-bottom: var(--sp-3)`).

**Gaps between tiles are the same as gaps inside them.** `.content` L162 uses `gap: var(--sp-3)` = 12px, and `--sp-3` is also the largest internal gap in the meter tile. Between-group space never exceeds within-group space — the single most load-bearing spacing rule, unmet.

## 2. Visual hierarchy — squint test
**Blurred, the #1 element is right: the 42px serif `.meter-val` (L180).** Nothing else in the popup exceeds 13px, so the focal point is unambiguous on size alone.

**But the #2 slot is contested, and colour is the reason.** `.meter-val.ok` renders in `--text-primary` (L184) while `.meter-verdict.ok` renders in `--success` green (L195). At `ok` state the *only* saturated element in the tile is the 13px verdict word — squinted, the green pip pulls as hard as the neutral 42px number. Then `.bar-fill` (L216) is also `--success`: two green marks, one 13px text and one 6px bar, saying the same thing twice while the hero says it in grey. At `warn`/`bad` the number does take colour (L185–186) and hierarchy resolves — so the design is strongest exactly when it matters least (calm state) and inconsistent across states.

**Whitespace does not guide the eye** — see §1; with no differentiated gaps the six meter lines read as a list, not as hero + supporting.

**Meter over trend: correct but under-asserted.** `#meterTile` gets a hairline (L172) and `--sp-4` padding; the trend tile is overridden to `padding:var(--sp-3)` inline (L512). Only 4px of padding and a 1px border carry a primacy that should be obvious.

## 3. Grid & structure
There is no grid — a 360×~520 single column, which is right. Structure is **inconsistent, not repetitive**: the two visible tiles are *not* identical. `#meterTile` = `.tile` + border + sp-4 padding, no heading. The trend = a heading `.sec-h` *outside* the tile (L504–511) with a `.tile` beneath it at sp-3 padding. `.extra-active` (L242) is a third pattern: not `.tile` at all — `--r-md` radius, `padding: var(--sp-3) var(--sp-4)`. `.settings-drawer` (L349) is a fourth: `.tile`-like but `--r-lg` with sp-3/sp-4 padding. So four card treatments across four blocks. Repetition would be *fine* here for product register; the problem is the opposite. Radius alone runs 12/8/12 across three sibling surfaces.

## 4. Rhythm & variety
No alternation. Everything is 4 or 12 with a scatter of 2/3/5/6. There is one genuine emphasis moment — the 42px serif against an all-11-and-under field, a good decision — and one deliberate visual break, the `--info` under-rule on `.spark-bar.today` (L284). Beyond those, sameness. `.trend-stats` (L300) at 10px sits `--sp-2` under the day labels, and `.trend-insight` `--sp-1` under that: three text lines at 10px stacked at near-identical intervals, an undifferentiated tail.

## 5. Density
**Right neighbourhood, wrong low end.** Overall footprint is appropriately tight for a 2-second glance. The failure is *fussy-tiny*: 10px is doing far too much work — `.meter-foot` (L223), `.meter-updated` (L226), `.spark-days` (L294), `.trend-stats` (L302), `.trend-insight` (L310, additionally *italic* at 10px on `--text-faint`, the weakest legibility combination in the file), `.export-btn` (L317), `.hourly-labels`, `.settings-hint`, `.poll-foot`. Nine distinct 10px roles at three colour values. Cutting text sizes rather than cutting content is how a glance tool gets slow.

# Systematic plan

## A. Spacing system (4pt base)
Replace the six props with: `--sp-1:4 --sp-2:8 --sp-3:12 --sp-4:16 --sp-5:24 --sp-6:32`. Retire 5px, 3px, 2px, and 6px from layout (keep 1–3px inside `.spark-bars`/`.hourly-bars` only, as data gaps).
- `.meter-val-row` → `gap: var(--sp-1)`, drop `margin-bottom` (let the verdict own the gap)
- `.export-btn` `padding: 2px 0` → `var(--sp-1) 0`; `.settings-row` `padding: 4px 0` → `var(--sp-1) 0`; `.settings-select` `padding: 3px 6px` → `var(--sp-1) var(--sp-2)`
- `.sec-h`/`.settings-toggle`/`.extra-active-label` `gap: 6px` → `var(--sp-2)`
- `.settings-hint`/`.hourly-labels`/`.spark-days` `margin-top: 2px|3px` → `var(--sp-1)`
- `.auth-state,.empty-state` `padding: var(--sp-6) var(--sp-5)` → `var(--sp-6) var(--sp-5)` reads as 32/24 under the new scale — unchanged in intent, now on-scale.

## B. Hierarchy strategy — three dimensions, in this order
1. **Size** (already carrying): 42 hero / 13 verdict / 11 support / 10 metadata. Hold the line; do not add a fifth step.
2. **Colour** (currently misassigned): move the state colour onto the hero. `.meter-val` takes `--success`/`--warning`/`--danger` in all three states; `.meter-verdict` drops to `--text-secondary` in every state and becomes a plain label. One saturated mark per tile — the number — plus the bar as its echo. This alone fixes the squint test.
3. **Space** (currently absent): see C. Do **not** recruit weight — 500/600 is already spread thin across `.meter-val-suffix`, `.meter-verdict`, `.sec-h`, `.meter-advisory`.

## C. Tight vs generous — concrete targets
Group the meter tile into three units and space *between* units, not within:
- **Unit 1 — the reading:** `.meter-val-row` + `.meter-verdict`. Internal: `4`. `.meter-verdict { margin: 0 0 var(--sp-3) }` (12, was 4).
- **Unit 2 — the mechanism:** `.meter-countdown` + `.bar-track` + `.meter-foot`. `.meter-countdown { margin-bottom: var(--sp-2) }` (8, was 12); `.bar-track { margin-bottom: var(--sp-1) }` (4, was 12 — the foot is the bar's axis and must sit on it).
- **Unit 3 — provenance:** `.meter-updated { margin-top: var(--sp-4) }` (16, was 4) — pushes the timestamp out of the data group. `.meter-advisory { margin-top: var(--sp-3) }`.
- **Between tiles:** `.content { gap: var(--sp-5) }` (24, was 12), so between-group > within-group by 2×.
- **Unify the surfaces:** delete the inline `padding:var(--sp-3)` on the trend tile (L512) → all `.tile` at `--sp-4`; make `.extra-active` and `.settings-drawer` use `.tile` (`--r-lg`, `--sp-4`) rather than three bespoke paddings/radii. Keep `#meterTile`'s border as the sole primacy signal.
- **Density fix:** raise `.meter-foot`, `.spark-days`, `.trend-stats` to 11px; drop `.trend-insight`'s italic; keep 10px for `.meter-updated`/`.poll-foot`/`.hourly-labels` only.
